\title{Uniform Herding: Exemplar Replay with Representation Refresh}
\author{}
\date{}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Replay must preserve prior classes as the feature representation changes, while
only a bounded active exemplar set can be replayed.  We propose Uniform
Herding, which allocates this active set across observed classes and refreshes
their selected exemplars in the current representation using a bounded
candidate pool.  On CIFAR-100 with ten class-incremental tasks, a ResNet-18
backbone, active budget $M=2{,}000$, retrieval budget $b=64$, and three seeds,
Uniform Herding obtains $44.99\pm1.00\%$ final average accuracy and
$13.85\pm0.44\%$ forgetting, compared with $42.36\pm0.87\%$ and
$19.46\pm0.10\%$ for iCaRL.  Within the Uniform Herding protocol, NME
prediction and herding selection each outperform their corresponding variant,
while distillation primarily reduces forgetting.  Over the tested
resource range, changing the active budget has a larger effect than changing
the retrieval budget.  These results characterize the method under the
evaluated protocol.  The iCaRL comparison is end-to-end and does not isolate
refresh from its other protocol differences.
\end{abstract}
